Receiver and exclusion zone

Enabled by default. When unchecked, positions older than the maximum position age are ignored, including an aircraft's last known position inside the zone. Ignoring a stale position does not confirm the aircraft has left. Aircraft with no usable position have a separate setting below. Lost tracks and receiver failures still request blackout.

Enabled by default. When unchecked, received aircraft records with missing or invalid coordinates or position age are ignored, even if their last known position was inside the zone. Their distance is unknown; ignoring them does not confirm they are outside the zone. Lost tracks, receiver failures and fresh aircraft inside the zone still request blackout. Save settings, then acknowledge any previous fault when available.

Public website dashboard

Off by default. Enabling publishes your display coordinates, zone radius, aircraft positions and blackout status to your website every 10 seconds. Visitors cannot change settings. Website outages do not delay local blackout monitoring.

Fixture blackout channels

Off = observation mode. On = actual DMX overrides. Click Save settings to apply checkbox changes. Verify coordinates, zone, channels and closed values before enabling.
